# Sample Datasets & Video Clips

Place your 10-second inspection video clips or test footage in this directory.

### Supported Video Formats:
- `.mp4`
- `.mov`
- `.avi`

### Recommended Folder Layout:
```text
datasets/
├── copper/         # Sample 10s clips of Copper wire, tubing, or coils
├── aluminum/       # Sample 10s clips of Aluminum extrusions, turnings, or cans
├── brass/          # Sample 10s clips of Brass scrap
├── steel/          # Sample 10s clips of Heavy Melt Steel (HMS) or rebar
└── mixed_debris/   # Sample clips with foreign matter, contamination, or occlusions
```

You can upload these clips directly through the dashboard UI at runtime.
